Baked Rice

Oven Baked Rice

Description

Baked rice is my favorite way to prepare rice! Can be easily doubled — just use a larger baking dish.

ingredients

  • 1 cup jasmine rice (such as Mahatma®)
  • 2 ¼ cups water
  • ¼ cup butter
  • 1 teaspoon salt
  • 1 teaspoon white vinegar

Steps

  1. Step1: Preheat the oven to 325 degrees F (165 degrees C). Gather all ingredients. Grease a 2 1/2-quart baking dish. /li>
  2. Step2: Pour rice into the prepared baking dish.
  3. Step3: Stir water, butter, salt, and vinegar together in a saucepan over medium heat.
  4. Step4: Bring to a rolling boil, then pour over rice; stir to combine.
  5. Step5: Bake in the preheated oven until water is absorbed and rice is tender, 20 to 25 minutes.
